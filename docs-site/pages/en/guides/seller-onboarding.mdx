# Seller Onboarding

Complete guide to become an OrignaGTA seller and start selling products.

## Requirements

Before you start, ensure you have:

- Valid email address
- Bank account (for payout via Stripe Connect)
- Government-issued ID for verification
- Business license (if applicable)
- Product inventory ready to list

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit [https://dev.orignagta.ca](https://dev.orignagta.ca)
2. Click **Sign Up** → **I'm a Seller**
3. Enter email and password
4. Click the verification link in your email

## Step 2: Complete Your Seller Profile

Navigate to **Settings** → **Seller Profile** and fill in:

- **Business Name** — Your shop name
- **Business Description** — What you sell
- **Phone Number** — For customer inquiries (optional)
- **Address** — Warehouse/fulfillment address
- **Return Policy** — Link to your return policy

## Step 3: Connect Stripe Account

To receive payments:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Payments**
2. Click **Connect Stripe Account**
3. You'll be redirected to Stripe OAuth
4. Authorize the connection
5. Return to OrignaGTA — your account is now live

**How payouts work:**
- Buyer purchases your product
- You ship the item
- Order marked as delivered → payout scheduled
- Stripe transfers funds to your bank account (typically 2–5 business days)

## Step 4: Create Your First Product

1. Click **Add Product** (or **+** button)
2. Fill in product details:
   - **Title** — Product name (required)
   - **Description** — Full details (required)
   - **Price** — In CAD dollars
   - **Stock Quantity** — How many available
   - **Category** — Choose from dropdown
   - **Images** — Upload up to 5 photos
3. Click **Save as Draft** to review later, or **Publish** to list immediately

### Product Tips

- **Be descriptive**: Include dimensions, materials, condition
- **High-quality photos**: Show product from multiple angles
- **Competitive pricing**: Check similar listings
- **Accurate stock**: Update as items sell
- **Fast shipping**: Set realistic delivery dates (impacts buyer ratings)

## Step 5: Manage Orders

Once orders arrive:

1. Go to **Orders** → **Incoming**
2. Confirm receipt of order (marks as "confirmed")
3. Prepare and ship item
4. Enter tracking number
5. Mark as **Shipped**
6. Buyer receives and confirms delivery
7. Funds arrive in your bank account

### Handling Returns

If a buyer requests a return:

1. Check return request in **Orders** → **Returns**
2. Review reason and condition
3. **Approve** to accept return and issue refund
4. **Reject** if outside return window or damaged by buyer
5. Buyer ships item back (provide return label)
6. Once received, confirm in system
7. Refund processed automatically

## Platform Fees

OrignaGTA charges a **small percentage** of each sale:

```
Sale Price (before tax)        $100.00 CAD
Platform Fee (%)               $  8.00 CAD (8%)
Seller Receives               $  92.00 CAD
```

Additional:
- **Stripe processing fee**: ~2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (deducted by Stripe, not OrignaGTA)
- **Shipping**: You set the shipping cost

## Best Practices

1. **Respond to messages quickly** — Buyers appreciate fast communication
2. **Ship promptly** — Update order status within 24 hours of purchase
3. **High-quality photos** — Products with 4+ photos sell better
4. **Clear descriptions** — Avoid returns from customer confusion
5. **Accurate pricing** — Include shipping in your price calculation
6. **Monitor inventory** — Out-of-stock items hurt your rating
