## English Version

### 1. Introduction

OrignaVentures Inc. ("**OrignaGTA**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains:

- What personal information we collect
- How we use, share, and protect that information
- Your rights regarding your personal information
- How to contact us with privacy questions

This policy applies to **orignaGTA.ca**, the OrignaGTA mobile application, and all services through which we provide our e-commerce marketplace (collectively, the "**Services**").

**This is a template. Please consult with a qualified Canadian privacy lawyer before deployment to ensure compliance with your specific operations and applicable law.**

---

### 2. What Personal Information We Collect

#### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use OrignaGTA, you may provide:

- **Account Information:** name, email address, phone number, password, profile photo, biographical information
- **Address Information:** shipping and billing address, postal code, province
- **Payment Information:** via Stripe (payment processor) — we do not directly collect credit card numbers; Stripe handles payment data processing
- **Communications:** messages you send to sellers or customer support, product reviews, feedback
- **Identification:** government-issued ID (for seller verification), tax identification number (for sellers), banking information (for payout setup)

#### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you interact with OrignaGTA, we automatically collect:

- **Usage Data:** pages visited, links clicked, time spent, search queries, products viewed, cart contents, orders placed
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, referrer URL
- **Cookies and Tracking:** Cloudflare Turnstile (bot protection), session cookies, analytics cookies (if applicable)
- **Location:** approximate location inferred from IP address (not precise GPS)
- **Error and Log Data:** crash reports, error messages, performance metrics (via Sentry error tracking)

#### 2.3 Information From Third Parties

We may receive information from:

- **Sellers:** when a seller lists you as a buyer in their seller account (order history, dispute history)
- **Payment Processors:** Stripe provides confirmation of payment status and transaction details
- **Shipping Partners:** tracking and delivery confirmation data
- **Third-Party Services:** identity verification services, fraud detection providers, analytics tools
- **Public Sources:** if you are a seller, publicly available information about your business may be collected for verification

### 3. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use personal information for:

#### 3.1 Providing Services

- Creating and maintaining your account
- Processing orders, payments, and refunds
- Arranging shipping and delivery
- Customer support and dispute resolution
- Personalization (showing relevant products based on browsing/purchase history)
- Search functionality and product recommendations

#### 3.2 Compliance and Legal Obligations

- Verifying seller identity and qualifications
- Complying with tax laws (sales tax collection, GST/HST, provincial requirements)
- Fraud prevention, security, and abuse detection
- Enforcing terms of service and legal agreements
- Responding to legal process and law enforcement requests
- Maintaining audit trails for financial and regulatory compliance

#### 3.3 Marketing and Communications (With Your Consent)

- Promotional emails and notifications (only with your express opt-in consent)
- Order and account notifications (transactional, not marketing)
- Product recommendations based on your activity
- Surveys and feedback requests

We will not use your information for marketing without your clear, separate consent. You can withdraw consent at any time via the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or by contacting us.

#### 3.4 Fraud Prevention and Security

- Analyzing suspicious activity patterns
- Verifying buyer and seller identities
- Detecting and preventing unauthorized access
- Protecting against fraud, chargebacks, and abuse

#### 3.5 Service Improvement

- Analyzing usage trends and user behavior
- Improving website/app functionality and user experience
- Testing new features
- Conducting aggregated analytics (anonymized data)

### 4. How We Share Your Personal Information

#### 4.1 Necessary for Transactions

When you place an order with a third-party seller, we share the information reasonably necessary to fulfill that order:

- **Seller:** your name, email, shipping address, phone number, order contents, purchase price
- **Shipping Partner:** name, address, phone, order details, tracking information
- **Stripe (Payment Processor):** payment amount, transaction ID, billing address, for payment processing

We disclose the minimum information necessary. Sellers are contractually bound to use this information only for order fulfillment and legal compliance.

#### 4.2 Service Providers and Processors

We engage third-party service providers who process personal information on our behalf:

| Service | Purpose | Location |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Stripe** | Payment processing, Stripe Connect (seller payouts) | USA |
| **Cloudflare** | CDN, DDoS protection, Turnstile bot protection | USA / Global |
| **Postal** | Transactional and marketing email delivery | USA / EU |
| **Sentry** | Error tracking and performance monitoring | USA |
| **Google Analytics** (optional) | Website traffic and user behavior analysis | USA |

All service providers are contractually required to use personal information only for their service purposes and to comply with PIPEDA and applicable privacy law.

#### 4.3 Cross-Border Data Transfers

**Important:** Some of our service providers are located in the United States. When you provide personal information to OrignaGTA, your data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States or other countries. These jurisdictions may have privacy protections different from those in Canada. By using OrignaGTA, you consent to this cross-border transfer.

#### 4.4 Legal Requirements and Law Enforcement

We may disclose personal information without consent if required or permitted by law:

- Responding to valid legal process (subpoena, court order, search warrant)
- Complying with law enforcement requests
- Protecting public safety or preventing imminent harm
- Enforcing our terms of service and other agreements
- Preventing fraud or abuse

#### 4.5 Business Transfers

If OrignaGTA is acquired, merged, or undergoes asset sale, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified if your personal information becomes subject to a materially different privacy policy.

#### 4.6 Aggregated and De-Identified Data

We may use aggregated or anonymized data (which cannot reasonably identify you) for analytics, benchmarking, marketing, and service improvement without restriction.

### 6. Your Privacy Rights Under PIPEDA

Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and provincial privacy laws, you have the right to:

#### 6.1 Right of Access

You can request access to personal information we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days with:
- A copy of your personal information
- Information about how it has been used
- A list of third parties it has been shared with

**How to Request:** Contact privacy@orignagta.ca with your name and account email.

#### 6.2 Right of Correction

You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information. We will update or correct within 30 days and notify any third parties who received the information.

**How to Request:** Update your profile directly in your account settings, or contact privacy@orignagta.ca.

#### 6.3 Right of Deletion

You can request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations:
- Account data: deleted within 30 days
- Tax/legal records: retained for 7 years as required by law

**How to Request:** Contact privacy@orignagta.ca with your name and account email.

#### 6.4 Right to Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw consent to marketing and promotional communications at any time by:
- Clicking "Unsubscribe" in any marketing email
- Updating notification preferences in your account settings
- Emailing privacy@orignagta.ca

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal or transactional emails.

#### 6.5 Right to Complain

If you believe we have violated your privacy rights, you may file a complaint with:

**Privacy Commissioner of Canada**
- **Website:** https://www.priv.gc.ca
- **Phone:** 1-800-282-1376
- **Mailing Address:** 30 Victoria Street, Gatineau, QC K1A 1H2

If you reside in Quebec, you may also file a complaint with:

**Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI)**
- **Website:** https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca
- **Phone:** 1-888-528-1333

### 7. Security and Safeguards

#### 7.1 Data Protection Measures

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information:

- **Encryption:** Data in transit uses TLS/SSL; sensitive data at rest is encrypted
- **Access Control:** Personal information access is limited to authorized employees and service providers
- **Authentication:** Passwords are hashed; multi-factor authentication available
- **Regular Audits:** Security assessments and penetration testing conducted periodically
- **Vendor Management:** Third-party processors undergo privacy and security assessments

#### 7.2 No Absolute Security

No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially reasonable security, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use OrignaGTA at your own risk.

#### 7.3 Breach Notification

If we discover a breach posing a real risk of significant harm to personal information, we will:
- Notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay
- Report to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required
- Provide information about the breach and recommended protective measures

### 8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

#### 8.1 Cloudflare Turnstile

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to verify that you are human and prevent bot abuse. Turnstile:
- Does not require CAPTCHA solving (frictionless)
- Collects minimal data: IP address, page URL, timestamp
- Does not track you across sites
- Privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

#### 8.2 Session Cookies

We use session cookies to maintain login state and provide functionality. These cookies:
- Expire when you close your browser
- Contain only a session identifier
- Do not track you across sites

#### 8.3 Third-Party Analytics (If Enabled)

If enabled, we may use Google Analytics to analyze usage trends:
- Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized
- You can opt out at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
