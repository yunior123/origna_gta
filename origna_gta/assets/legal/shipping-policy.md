# Shipping Policy / Politique d'Expédition

**Effective Date / Date d'entrée en vigueur:** March 18, 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** Canada

---

## English Version

### 1. Overview

This Shipping Policy explains how OrignaGTA and its sellers handle shipping for orders placed through the OrignaGTA marketplace. Each seller sets its own shipping rates, methods, and timelines, subject to the minimum standards outlined below.

---

### 2. Shipping Coverage and Areas

#### 2.1 Geographic Coverage

OrignaGTA operates a marketplace where sellers from across Canada can sell to buyers across Canada. Sellers may choose to ship to:

- All of Canada (all provinces and territories)
- Specific provinces or regions (seller's choice)
- Specific postal code ranges (seller's choice)

Sellers clearly indicate their shipping area during product listing. Buyers can filter by shipping availability to their location.

#### 2.2 International Shipping (Future)

Currently, OrignaGTA supports domestic (Canada-wide) shipping only. International shipping may be available in the future; when offered, it will be seller-specific and clearly marked.

---

### 3. Shipping Timeline

#### 3.1 Seller Processing Time

Sellers must ship orders within **3 business days** of order confirmation. This means:

- **Order confirmed:** Payment authorized and order appears in seller dashboard
- **3 business days:** Excludes weekends and statutory holidays
- **Shipping deadline:** Seller must hand off package to carrier by end of business day on Day 3

**Example:** Order confirmed Monday 9:00 AM → Seller ships by Wednesday 5:00 PM.

#### 3.2 Carrier Delivery Time

Once the seller ships the order, delivery time depends on the shipping method selected:

| Shipping Method | Typical Delivery Time | Carrier |
|-----------------|----------------------|---------|
| **Standard Shipping** | 3–10 business days | Canada Post, regional couriers |
| **Expedited Shipping** | 2–5 business days | UPS, FedEx, priority couriers |
| **Express Shipping** | Next day or 2-day | FedEx, UPS Express, regional express |
| **Local Pickup** | Same day or next day | Seller warehouse or designated location |

Delivery times are estimates and vary by:
- Seller location
- Buyer location
- Weather, holidays, carrier capacity
- Customs delays (if applicable)

OrignaGTA and sellers are not liable for carrier delays beyond their control.

#### 3.3 Delivery Confirmation

An order is considered **delivered** when:

1. **Carrier confirms delivery** at the address provided (tracking shows "Delivered"), OR
2. **7 days have passed** since tracking shows the item arrived in the destination city (auto-delivery without signature)

Buyers can confirm delivery manually in their account or it auto-confirms after 7 days.

---

### 4. Shipping Rates and Fees

#### 4.1 Seller-Set Rates

Each seller determines its own shipping rates based on:
- Seller's location (affects carrier rates)
- Buyer's location and distance
- Package weight and dimensions
- Shipping method selected (standard, expedited, express)
- Carrier used (Canada Post, UPS, FedEx, regional)

Sellers must display shipping costs **before checkout**. Buyers can see:
- Shipping cost for their postal code
- Shipping method and estimated delivery time
- Tracking availability

#### 4.2 Free Shipping Threshold

Orders with a **subtotal of $75 CAD or more** may qualify for **free shipping** (if the seller offers it).

- **Subtotal:** Product price only, **before tax and shipping**
- **Applies to:** Eligible categories and sellers
- **Seller choice:** Not all sellers may offer free shipping; check product listing

**Example:**
- Product price: $80 → Subtotal $80 (≥$75) → Free shipping eligible
- Product price: $100 + Tax: $13 = Total $113, but Subtotal $100 → Free shipping eligible
- Product price: $70 → Subtotal $70 (<$75) → Free shipping not available

#### 4.3 Multiple Items from Same Seller

If a buyer purchases multiple items from the same seller in one order:

- Shipping is charged **once per order** (not per item)
- Seller may offer combined shipping discount
- All items ship together unless specified separately

#### 4.4 Multiple Sellers

If a buyer purchases items from multiple sellers:

- Each seller's order ships separately
- Buyer pays shipping **per seller**, not per order
- Example: 2 sellers = 2 separate shipments and 2 shipping charges (unless free shipping threshold applies)

---

### 5. Shipping Methods and Tracking

#### 5.1 Tracking Availability

All orders must include **tracking**. Buyers receive:

- **Tracking number** via email when seller ships
- **Tracking link** to carrier website (Canada Post, UPS, FedEx, etc.)
- **In-app tracking** on their OrignaGTA order page

Sellers are responsible for providing accurate, functional tracking information.

#### 5.2 Carrier Tracking

Buyers can track packages directly:

- **Canada Post:** Track.canadapost.ca
- **UPS:** tracking.ups.com
- **FedEx:** fedex.com/tracking
- **Regional Couriers:** Varies by courier (sellers provide links)

#### 5.3 Shipping Label and Insurance

- **Shipping Label:** Included in price (seller provides)
- **Insurance:** Optional; seller may offer or require buyer to purchase
- **Signature Required:** Seller may require signature on delivery (buyer is notified)

---

### 6. Special Shipping Categories

#### 6.1 Perishable Items (Food, Beverages, Temperature-Sensitive)

Sellers offering perishable or temperature-sensitive products must:

- **Use Expedited Shipping:** Next-day or 2-day service minimum
- **Insulation:** Include appropriate insulation and refrigerants (ice packs, gel packs)
- **Protective Packaging:** Prevent damage and contamination
- **Clear Instructions:** Include handling and storage instructions
- **Insurance:** Strongly recommended for high-value perishables
- **Delivery Window:** Target delivery within 24–48 hours of shipment

**Spoilage Liability:**
- Seller is responsible for spoilage caused by inadequate packaging or slow shipping
- Buyer should report spoilage within **14 days** of delivery
- Refund eligibility depends on product condition and evidence

**OrignaGTA Responsibility:**
- OrignaGTA does not guarantee perishable item quality
- Buyer accepts risk of spoilage from carrier delays beyond seller/OrignaGTA control

#### 6.2 Fragile Items (Glass, Ceramics, Sculptures)

Sellers offering fragile items must:

- **Protective Packaging:** Bubble wrap, foam, cardboard layers
- **Proper Boxing:** Use sturdy boxes; do not reuse damaged packaging
- **Labeling:** Mark box "FRAGILE" and include handling instructions
- **Insurance:** Recommended for items >$50 CAD
- **Signature:** May require signature on delivery

#### 6.3 Hazardous Materials

Sellers selling hazardous materials (cleaning supplies, paints, chemicals) must:

- **Comply with Transport Canada regulations** for hazardous goods shipping
- **Carrier Certification:** Only use carriers certified to ship hazardous materials
- **Labeling:** Follow Transport Canada hazard labeling (Class labels, UN numbers)
- **Documentation:** Provide safety data sheets (SDS) with shipment if required
- **Restricted Routes:** Some carriers may refuse certain hazardous materials

**OrignaGTA Prohibition:** Some hazardous materials are prohibited on the platform (see Terms of Service).

#### 6.4 Oversize/Heavy Items

Items exceeding **50 lbs (23 kg)** or **100 cm on any dimension**:

- **Shipping Cost:** May be significantly higher than standard
- **Carrier Availability:** Some carriers may refuse; seller responsible for finding suitable carrier
- **Delivery:** May require special delivery arrangements (commercial address, scheduled delivery)
- **Buyer Notification:** Seller must clearly indicate item size/weight and any special delivery requirements

---

### 7. Non-Delivery and Damage Claims

#### 7.1 Non-Delivery

If a package doesn't arrive within the expected delivery window:

**Buyer Action (within 14 days of expected delivery):**
1. Check tracking status (may be in transit or delayed)
2. Contact seller or OrignaGTA support with order ID and tracking number
3. OrignaGTA investigates with carrier

**Resolution:**
- If carrier confirms lost package: Seller/OrignaGTA initiates claim with carrier and issues refund or replacement
- If carrier shows delivery attempted: Buyer may need to contact carrier or arrange redelivery
- If carrier is slow: No refund until 7+ days past estimated delivery (auto-delivery trigger)

#### 7.2 Damaged Goods

If a package arrives damaged:

**Buyer Action (within 14 days of delivery):**
1. Document damage with photos (packaging and product)
2. Do NOT discard packaging; keep for claim evidence
3. Contact seller or OrignaGTA support with photos and order details

**Resolution:**
- **Seller Responsibility:** If damage is due to inadequate packaging (seller's fault), seller refunds or replaces
- **Carrier Claim:** If damage is due to carrier mishandling, seller or buyer files claim with carrier
- **OrignaGTA Role:** Mediates disputes between buyer and seller; may require shipping insurance claim

**Claim Deadline:** Buyers must report damage within **14 days** of delivery or forfeit claim eligibility.

#### 7.3 Shipping Delays

OrignaGTA and sellers are **not liable** for delays caused by:

- Carrier delays (weather, strikes, capacity issues)
- Customs or border delays (if applicable)
- Statutory holidays or weekends
- Natural disasters or force majeure events
- Incorrect address provided by buyer

**Buyer Recourse:** If delay is unreasonable (>2 weeks past estimated delivery), buyer may request cancellation and refund.

---

### 8. Return Shipping and Refunds

#### 8.1 Return Shipping Responsibility

The policy for return shipping costs depends on the reason for return:

| Reason | Buyer Pays | Seller Pays |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **Change of mind / buyer regret** | Buyer (unless seller offers free returns) | — |
| **Defective / not as described** | Seller | ✓ Seller may provide prepaid return label |
| **Wrong item sent** | Seller | ✓ Seller must provide prepaid return label |
| **Damaged in transit** | Seller/Carrier claim | Seller arranges replacement |

#### 8.2 Return Process

1. Buyer initiates return request within **30 days** of delivery
2. Seller reviews return request (accepts or rejects)
3. If accepted, seller may provide prepaid return shipping label
4. Buyer ships item back to seller
5. Seller receives, inspects, and issues refund within **5 business days**

#### 8.3 Refund Timeline

- **Refund Issued:** Within 5 business days of seller receiving return
- **Refund Appears:** 5–10 business days after issued (depends on buyer's bank/payment processor)

---

### 9. Customs and Border (Cross-Province)

#### 9.1 Interprovincial Shipping

Most items ship freely across provinces. However:

- **Restricted Items:** Some provinces restrict certain products (e.g., alcohol, cannabis, ammunition)
- **Seller Responsibility:** Seller ensures products are legal in destination province
- **Buyer Responsibility:** Buyer ensures they are legally allowed to receive the item

#### 9.2 Perishable Cross-Province Rules

Perishable items (food, plants with soil) may be restricted:

- **Prohibited Routes:** Some sellers restrict cross-province perishable shipping
- **Health Canada Rules:** Fresh produce, meat, dairy subject to health regulations
- **Buyer Notification:** Listing clearly indicates if perishables cannot ship to specific provinces

---

### 10. Seller Obligations

Each seller shipping through OrignaGTA must:

- [ ] Ship orders within **3 business days** of confirmation
- [ ] Provide accurate, functional **tracking numbers**
- [ ] Use appropriate **packaging** (padding, insulation, labeling)
- [ ] Display **shipping costs** before checkout
- [ ] Comply with **carrier regulations** (hazardous materials, oversized items, etc.)
- [ ] Respond to **damage/loss claims** within 48 hours
- [ ] Process **returns** and issue refunds within 5 business days
- [ ] Provide **tracking updates** in app
- [ ] Not overcharge for shipping (charges must reflect actual carrier costs + reasonable handling fee)

Sellers failing to meet these obligations may face:
- Buyer complaints and negative reviews
- OrignaGTA account suspension
- Mandatory refund to buyer
- Removal from marketplace

---

### 11. Contact and Disputes

For shipping questions or disputes:

**OrignaGTA Support**
- **Email:** support@orignagta.ca
- **Response Time:** Within 24 hours
- **Dispute Resolution:** OrignaGTA mediates between buyer and seller; decision is binding

## Version Française / French Version

### 1. Aperçu

Cette Politique d'Expédition explique comment OrignaGTA et ses vendeurs gèrent l'expédition des commandes effectuées sur la place de marché OrignaGTA. Chaque vendeur fixe ses propres tarifs, méthodes et délais d'expédition, conformément aux normes minimales énoncées ci-dessous.

### 2. Couverture et Zones de Livraison

#### 2.1 Couverture Géographique

OrignaGTA opère une place de marché où les vendeurs de partout au Canada peuvent vendre aux acheteurs de partout au Canada. Les vendeurs peuvent choisir d'expédier vers :

- Tout le Canada (toutes les provinces et territoires)
- Des provinces ou régions spécifiques (au choix du vendeur)
- Des plages de codes postaux spécifiques (au choix du vendeur)

### 3. Délai d'Expédition

#### 3.1 Délai de Traitement du Vendeur

Les vendeurs doivent expédier les commandes dans les **3 jours ouvrables** suivant la confirmation de la commande. Cela signifie :

- **Commande confirmée :** Paiement autorisé et commande apparaît dans le tableau de bord du vendeur
- **3 jours ouvrables :** Exclut les fins de semaine et les jours fériés
- **Délai d'expédition :** Le vendeur doit remettre le colis au transporteur avant 17 h le jour 3

#### 3.2 Délai de Livraison du Transporteur

| Méthode d'Expédition | Délai Typique | Transporteur |
|---------------------|---------------|------------|
| **Expédition Standard** | 3–10 jours ouvrables | Postes Canada, courriers régionaux |
| **Expédition Accélérée** | 2–5 jours ouvrables | UPS, FedEx, courriers prioritaires |
| **Expédition Express** | Jour suivant ou 2 jours | FedEx, UPS Express, express régionaux |
| **Retrait Local** | Jour même ou jour suivant | Entrepôt du vendeur ou lieu désigné |

### 4. Tarifs et Frais d'Expédition

#### 4.1 Tarifs Fixés par le Vendeur

Chaque vendeur détermine ses propres tarifs en fonction de :
- Localisation du vendeur (affecte les tarifs des transporteurs)
- Localisation et distance de l'acheteur
- Poids et dimensions du colis
- Méthode d'expédition sélectionnée
- Transporteur utilisé

#### 4.2 Seuil de Livraison Gratuite

Les commandes d'un **sous-total de 75 $ CAD ou plus** peuvent bénéficier de la **livraison gratuite** (si le vendeur l'offre).

- **Sous-total :** Seul le prix du produit, **avant taxes et frais de port**

#### 4.3 Articles de Plusieurs Vendeurs

Si un acheteur achète des articles de plusieurs vendeurs :

- Chaque commande de vendeur expédie séparément
- L'acheteur paie l'expédition **par vendeur**, pas par commande

### 5. Méthodes d'Expédition et Suivi

#### 5.1 Disponibilité du Suivi

Tous les commandes incluent le **suivi**. Les acheteurs reçoivent :

- **Numéro de suivi** par e-mail quand le vendeur expédie
- **Lien de suivi** vers le site Web du transporteur
- **Suivi dans l'app** sur sa page de commande OrignaGTA

#### 5.2 Articles Périssables

Les vendeurs proposant des articles périssables ou sensibles à la température doivent :

- **Utiliser l'Expédition Accélérée :** Service jour suivant ou 2 jours minimum
- **Isolation :** Inclure l'isolation appropriée et les réfrigérants (packs de glace)
- **Emballage Protecteur :** Prévenir les dommages et la contamination
- **Instructions Claires :** Inclure les instructions de manipulation et de stockage
- **Assurance :** Fortement recommandée pour les articles périssables de grande valeur

### 6. Non-Livraison et Réclamations pour Dommages

#### 6.1 Non-Livraison

Si un colis n'arrive pas dans la fenêtre de livraison attendue :

**Action de l'Acheteur (dans les 14 jours suivant la livraison attendue) :**
1. Vérifier le statut du suivi
2. Contacter le vendeur ou le support OrignaGTA
3. OrignaGTA enquête auprès du transporteur

#### 6.2 Marchandises Endommagées

Si un colis arrive endommagé :

**Action de l'Acheteur (dans les 14 jours suivant la livraison) :**
1. Documenter les dommages avec des photos
2. Ne pas jeter l'emballage ; conserver pour preuves
3. Contacter le vendeur ou le support OrignaGTA
