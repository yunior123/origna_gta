# Quebec Bill 96 — French Language Compliance Checklist

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2025  
**Jurisdiction:** Quebec, Canada  
**Law:** Bill 96: An Act respecting French, the official and common language of Quebec  
**Regulatory Authority:** Office Québécois de la Langue Française (OQLF)

---

## 1. Overview

Quebec's **Bill 96** reinforces French language requirements for businesses operating in Quebec, including digital services, e-commerce platforms, and online marketplaces. As of **June 1, 2025**, OrignaGTA must ensure:

- All customer-facing content is available in French
- French is given equal or greater prominence than English
- Product descriptions by sellers are encouraged/required in French for Quebec sales
- Customer support is available in French
- Commercial documents (ToS, Privacy Policy, etc.) are provided in French first

**Failure to comply may result in warnings, administrative fines (up to CAD $7,000 per violation for organizations), or legal action by the OQLF.**

---

## 2. Legal Framework

### 2.1 Charter of the French Language (Bill 96)

**Article 52 — Web Content:**
- All public content must be available in French, regardless of medium (digital, printed, signage)
- French must be given equal treatment to other languages in terms of presentation and accessibility
- Language cannot be a barrier to using a service or accessing information

**Article 54 — Adhesion Contracts (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy):**
- Contracts of adhesion must be presented in French first
- Only **after** the user reads/accepts the French version can they elect to review/proceed in another language
- Both versions must have equal legal weight

**Article 215 — Commerce:**
- Goods and services advertised to consumers in Quebec must be accompanied by French descriptions
- English versions cannot be more prominent or detailed than French versions

**Article 97–100 — Trademark and Product Names:**
- Non-French trademarks and product names must be accompanied by French generic terms (descriptive text)
- "OrignaGTA" (English) must be accompanied by generic descriptor in French
- Example: OrignaGTA — Marché d'achat-vente en ligne (Online marketplace)

### 2.2 Key Changes as of June 1, 2025

- Increased enforcement by OQLF
- Stricter interpretation of "equal prominence" (not just availability, but visual parity)
- Product name/trademark rules now apply to digital product listings (previously ambiguous)
- Distance contract rules now explicitly apply to digital services

## 3. OrignaGTA French Language Compliance Checklist

### 3.1 Website and Mobile App

- [ ] **Homepage:** French version at least as prominent as English (equal font size, loading time, navigation placement)
- [ ] **Navigation Menu:** All menu items in French, with English toggle visible but secondary
- [ ] **Product Pages:** Product title, description, price, seller info, reviews all in French (or auto-translated at minimum)
- [ ] **Product Search:** Search results display French product titles by default when user's language preference is French
- [ ] **Shopping Cart:** Cart labels, pricing, shipping options in French
- [ ] **Checkout:** All steps (address, payment, order review) in French
- [ ] **Account Creation:** Registration form, password reset, account settings in French
- [ ] **Seller Onboarding:** Seller signup, product listing form, seller dashboard in French
- [ ] **Help/Support:** FAQ, help articles, contact page in French
- [ ] **Error Messages:** All error messages, alerts, notifications in French
- [ ] **Emails:** Order confirmations, shipping updates, account notifications in French
- [ ] **Footer:** Links to Privacy Policy (French version), ToS (French version), Contact in French

### 3.2 Legal Documents

- [ ] **Privacy Policy:** French version posted at `/privacy-policy-fr.html` or `/confidentialité.html`
- [ ] **Terms of Service:** French version posted at `/terms-of-service-fr.html` or `/conditions-utilisation.html`
- [ ] **Seller Agreement:** French version provided to all Quebec sellers
- [ ] **Shipping Policy:** French version available (if separate document)
- [ ] **Return Policy:** French version available
- [ ] **First Presentation:** French version presented first on legal document pages, with language toggle below
- [ ] **Equal Content:** French and English versions have identical legal terms and conditions (no omissions in French)

### 3.3 E-Commerce Transaction Flow

- [ ] **Product Listing (Quebec Sellers):** Require French product titles and descriptions for Quebec-facing listings
  - Example: Can sellers post English-only? **No** — at minimum, provide auto-translation or require French entry
- [ ] **Sold By / Shipped By:** Seller information in French when seller is located in Quebec
- [ ] **Product Reviews:** Allow reviews in both French and English; display French reviews prominently if user language is French
- [ ] **Seller Messaging:** Buyer-seller in-app messages support both French and English (no language barrier)
- [ ] **Invoice/Receipt:** Provide option to generate invoices in French for Quebec purchases

### 3.4 Seller Compliance Requirements

- [ ] **Seller Onboarding:** Require sellers to agree to provide French product information for Quebec-facing listings
  - Suggested text: "Pour vendre au Québec, veuillez fournir des descriptions de produits en français ou en anglais avec traduction en français."
  - ("To sell in Quebec, please provide product descriptions in French or in English with French translation.")
- [ ] **Product Listing Form:** Include toggle: "Is this product available in Quebec?" → If yes, French description is required (or provide auto-translation option)
- [ ] **Seller Dashboard:** Display option to add/edit French translations of product descriptions
- [ ] **Enforcement:** Flag listings without French descriptions if seller has Quebec as a service area

### 3.5 Customer Support

- [ ] **Support Email:** support@orignagta.ca responds to French-language inquiries in French within 24 hours
- [ ] **Phone Support (if offered):** Offer French-language support line or bilingual option
- [ ] **Live Chat (if offered):** Offer French-speaking agent or French language option
- [ ] **FAQ:** Available in both French and English with equal navigation prominence
- [ ] **Accessibility:** No barriers to accessing support in French (no additional fees, no delays)

### 3.6 Marketing and Promotions

- [ ] **Social Media:** Post promotional content in French on Quebec-facing channels (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
- [ ] **Email Campaigns:** Marketing emails to Quebec subscribers sent in French (unless user opts for English)
- [ ] **In-App Banners:** Promotional banners in French for French-language users
- [ ] **Newsletter:** Offer French-language newsletter option; both versions equally prominent
- [ ] **Advertising:** Any paid advertising targeting Quebec is in French or bilingual

### 3.7 Trademark and Branding

- [ ] **OrignaGTA Logo:** Include generic descriptor in French on marketing materials and website
  - Example: "OrignaGTA — Place de marché en ligne" (OrignaGTA — Online marketplace)
- [ ] **Product Names:** Non-French product names display alongside French generic descriptors
  - Example: "Apple iPhone 15 — Téléphone intelligent" (Smart phone)
- [ ] **Seller Branding:** If sellers use non-French trademarks, provide guidance on adding French generic terms

## 4. Accessibility and User Experience Standards

### 4.1 Equal Prominence

French content must be displayed with **equal visibility** to English:

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Font Size** | French text same size as English text |
| **Font Color** | French text same color contrast as English text |
| **Loading Time** | French pages load at same speed as English pages |
| **Navigation Position** | French links in same position as English links (not below the fold) |
| **Mobile View** | French version fully functional on mobile; not hidden behind extra taps |
| **Search Results** | French product titles appear if user language is set to French |
| **Sorting/Filtering** | French-language filters work identically to English filters |

### 4.2 Language Selection (Default and Persistent)

- [ ] **Auto-Detect:** Detect user location (Quebec IP) and default to French
- [ ] **Language Toggle:** Visible in header; allows switching to English
- [ ] **Persistence:** Remember user's language choice across sessions (store in account/cookie)
- [ ] **No Friction:** Switching languages takes 1 click, not multiple steps

### 4.3 Content Quality Standards

- [ ] **Machine Translation:** If using auto-translation, review Quebec French fluency
  - Use: Google Translate, DeepL (acceptable for marketplace descriptions)
  - Avoid: Overly literal or nonsensical translations
- [ ] **Human Translation:** For legal documents, use professional translator
  - Cost: ~$0.10–0.15 CAD per word; budget ~$500–1000 for Privacy Policy + ToS
- [ ] **Canadian French Dialect:** Use Quebec/Canadian French spelling and terminology
  - Examples: "courriel" (not "email"), "téléphone intelligent" (not "cellulaire")

## 5. Seller and Marketplace Content Guidelines

### 5.1 Product Descriptions

**Current State:** OrignaGTA has `en.json` + `fr.json` translation files for UI.

**Gap:** Third-party seller product descriptions are not automatically translated.

**Compliance Options:**

**Option A: Require French Descriptions (Strictest)**
- Sellers must provide French product titles and descriptions for Quebec listings
- Sellers can toggle "Available in Quebec?" when listing
- If yes, require French entry or provide auto-translate button
- Enforcement: Flag/suppress listings without French descriptions in Quebec

**Option B: Auto-Translate with Seller Option (Recommended)**
- Auto-translate seller descriptions to French using Google Translate API
- Display translated version with "[Translated]" label in app
- Sellers can edit/improve translations in their dashboard
- No enforcement; seller cooperation is incentivized but not required

**Option C: English-Only with Disclaimer (Not Compliant)**
- Keep seller descriptions in English only
- Add disclaimer: "Available in English only" in French
- **⚠️ Not compliant with Bill 96** — increases OQLF risk

**Recommendation:** Use **Option B** (auto-translate + seller opt-in refinement) as a balance between compliance and seller burden.

### 5.2 Seller Agreements

For all sellers targeting Quebec (indicated by "Available in Quebec?" toggle):

```
Entente de Conformité Linguistique du Québec / Quebec Language Compliance Agreement

En vendant des produits au Québec par l'entremise de la Place de Marché OrignaGTA, 
vous acceptez de respecter les exigences de la Loi 96 (Bill 96) :

By selling products in Quebec through the OrignaGTA Marketplace, you agree to comply 
with Bill 96 (Law 96) requirements:

- Product descriptions will be displayed in French to Quebec customers
- Descriptions may be auto-translated; you can review and edit translations
- You agree not to advertise misleading or French-language violations
- OrignaGTA reserves the right to suspend listings that violate Quebec language law

☐ I understand and agree to comply with Quebec language requirements
```

## 6. OQLF Compliance Metrics and Monitoring

### 6.1 Monthly Compliance Audit

OrignaGTA conducts audits to ensure ongoing compliance:

| Metric | Target | Method | Frequency |
|--------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **French Homepage Coverage** | 100% | Automated text extraction | Monthly |
| **French Product Listing %** | ≥90% in Quebec | Query: Count products with French title + description | Monthly |
| **French Language Toggle Visible** | Yes, in <2 clicks | Manual test on home/product/checkout pages | Monthly |
| **French Legal Docs Available** | Yes | Check Privacy Policy, ToS French URLs | Quarterly |
| **French Support Response Time** | <24 hours | Track support emails with French language identifier | Monthly |
| **Mobile French UX** | Fully functional | Test on iOS/Android, French language | Quarterly |

### 6.2 Incident Response (OQLF Complaint)

If OQLF issues a warning or complaint about French compliance:

1. **Acknowledgment:** Respond to OQLF within 10 business days
2. **Assessment:** Audit the specific allegation (e.g., "French legal documents not available")
3. **Remediation:** Fix issue within 30 days (e.g., translate missing documents)
4. **Documentation:** Send proof of remediation to OQLF (screenshots, translations)
5. **Preventive:** Update processes to avoid recurrence (e.g., add translation checklist for all new features)
