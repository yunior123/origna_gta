Stripe charges payment processing fees (~2.9% + $0.30 CAD per transaction). **OrignaGTA absorbs these costs** on behalf of sellers; sellers do not pay Stripe fees directly.

#### 3.3 No Additional Fees

OrignaGTA charges no monthly subscription fees, listing fees, or other hidden charges. The only charge is the 5% platform commission on completed orders.

#### 3.5 Seller Payout Schedule

Sellers receive payouts via Stripe Connect after an order reaches **delivered** status, typically within 2–5 business days. Payouts reflect:
